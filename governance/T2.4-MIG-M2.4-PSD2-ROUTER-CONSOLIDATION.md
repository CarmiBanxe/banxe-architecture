## Executive Summary

**Discovery finding:** three distinct PSD2 router implementations exist in `banxe-emi-stack`:

1. **Candidate A: `services/open_banking/`** (IL-OBK-01, Phase 15)
   - AISP/PISP consent lifecycle + payment initiation (OBIE/PSD2)
   - Status: ACTIVE, mounted, 15+ tests
   - Blocker: PISP mock, not wired to M2.1 PaymentEnginePort

2. **Candidate B: `services/psd2_gateway/`** (IL-PSD2GW-01, Phase 52B, adorsys XS2A)
   - Live CAMT.053 bank statement auto-pull via adorsys
   - Status: ACTIVE, mounted, 10+ tests
   - Key: IBAN blocking (I-02), DecimalString (I-01), HITL L4 (I-27)

3. **Candidate C: `services/statements/`** (S17-07)
   - Client statement generation (PDF/CSV), FCA PS7/24
   - Status: ACTIVE, mounted, 8+ tests
   - **Orthogonal:** client reporting, not PSD2 provider surface

**Key finding:** A ↔ B overlap in PSD2 provider surface. C is separate (client reporting).

**Recommendation:** **Canonical = B (`psd2_gateway`)**. Rationale: adorsys XS2A live integration, CAMT.053 auto-pull (P0 safeguarding), HITL-gated (FCA required), newer architecture (Phase 52B). Retire A after porting unique slices (PISP, TPP registry, SCA, CBPII) to B or M2.5.

**C → KEEP separate** (non-overlapping domain).

## Recommendation

### Winner: Candidate B (`psd2_gateway`)

**Rationale:**

1. **P0 safeguarding critical:** CAMT.053 auto-pull is blocking daily reconciliation (IL-SAF-01). B has it; A doesn't.

2. **Production-ready:** B is FCA-wired (HITL L4, I-01, I-02, I-27 all explicit). A is HITL-ready but not enforced.

3. **Newer architecture:** Phase 52B (B) incorporates learnings from Phase 15 (A).

4. **Clear single responsibility:** B = "read account data via PSD2 bank API". A = "manage consents + initiate payments" (wider scope).

### Treatment:

| Candidate | Decision | Action |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **A** | ARCHIVE | Port PISP/TPP/CBPII to B (M2.4a–c) or M2.5. Retire after. |
| **B** | CANONICAL | Keep. Expand to absorb unique slices from A. |
| **C** | KEEP | No consolidation (separate domain). |
