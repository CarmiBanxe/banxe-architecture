# Product Evidence Pack Template — Sprint 3

Status: TEMPLATE / NOT FOR MERGE

## Purpose
Стандартизированный evidence pack на продукт: выравнивает product governance с четырьмя исходами Consumer Duty (products & services, price & value, communications, support). Гейт H-017 (CEO) + заполненный pack = COMBINED requirement.

## Scope (секции экземпляра пака)
1. Product overview: name · type · manufacturer/distributor roles (bank/partner/co-man) · licence assumed (cross-check в Permissions Map).
2. Target market statement: profile · negative target market · vulnerable customers (design+support). «Beyond generic labels».
3. Fair value assessment: таблица segment/benefits/total cost/fair-value-view/evidence + pricing methodology + cross-subsidies/bundling.
4. Distribution & communications: channels · suitability controls per channel · KFS/T&C/marketing (COBS4→MLRO для промо) · vulnerable-comms readability.
5. Outcomes & monitoring: KPI/threshold/owner/cadence (complaints rate, usage, drop-off, arrears) + feedback в дизайн/комитеты.
6. Complaints & escalation: routes (F1/support DISP-контур) · темы жалоб → изменения продукта.
7. Evidence & governance: комитет-минуты · periodic reviews · board-reporting.
